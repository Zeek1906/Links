Phase 02 Customize Your Platform
✓
03
Set Your Donation Limits
▶ Video 1:49
›
From your back office, go to Donation Settings. Here you choose which donation types to offer: One-Off (single gift), Regular (recurring), and Round-Up (spare change rounded up from purchases). You can set the suggested dollar amounts donors will see for each type.
Tip For PTA and school-based campaigns, we recommend enabling all three. Round-Up is especially powerful — parents set it once and it runs automatically.

Create Your First Event
▶ Video 2:15
›
Events let you run targeted campaigns tied to specific fundraising moments — a school carnival, a book fair, a fundraiser dinner. Go to Events from the back office menu, tap Create New Event, and fill in your event name, description, image, donation amounts, and dates.
Tip You don't need a physical event to create one. A "Back to School Drive" or "Spring Campaign" works just as well. Events give donors a reason to give right now.

Download Your QR Code
▶ Video 0:53
›
Your QR code is a direct link to your donation page. Anyone who scans it goes straight to your giving options. Download it from the back office menu and save it. You'll use this on flyers, in emails, at events, on your website, and anywhere your community sees it.
Tip Print it on a sign for your next school meeting. Put it in your email signature. The more places it appears, the more donors you reach without any extra work.
