Welcome from Beacon — Start Here
Getting Started
What This Step Is For

Before you touch anything in your back office, this step makes sure you understand what RAYZIT is, how the platform works, and what you need to have ready. Starting without preparation is the most common reason people get stuck.

What You Need Before You Begin
  • 1Your organization's designated admin email address. This is the email you will use to log in to your RAYZIT back office every time. Choose one that will not change.
  • 2An active Stripe account for your organization. Stripe is the payment processor RAYZIT uses to move donations from your donors to your bank account. If you do not have one yet, go to stripe.com and create one before continuing. Stripe verification typically takes 1 to 2 business days.
TipDo not skip ahead if your Stripe account is not ready. Without Stripe connected, no donations can process and you will have to come back to this before going live.

Create Your Admin Username and Password
Account Setup
What This Step Is For

This is how you and your team will log in to the RAYZIT back office going forward. Your back office is where you manage everything — donation settings, events, QR codes, brand settings, and donation history.

How to Create Your Login
  • 1Open the RAYZIT back office link that was sent to you in your welcome email.
  • 2Enter your organization's admin email address in the email field. Use the same email you identified in Step 1.
  • 3Create a secure password. Use something your team can remember but that is not easy to guess.
  • 4Confirm your password and submit. Your account is now created.
  • 5Write down or save your email and password somewhere safe. Your team may need access and a reset can delay your launch.
TipWe recommend training at least two people at your organization on the back office login. Single-contact accounts are the most common reason organizations lose momentum.

3
Connect RAYZIT to Your Stripe Account
Account Setup
What This Step Is For

Stripe is how donations reach your bank account. Until Stripe is connected, your RAYZIT page is live but no money can move. This is a required step before you can accept any donations.

How to Connect Stripe
  • 1Log in to your RAYZIT back office using the credentials you created in Step 2.
  • 2In the left side menu, tap or click Connect Stripe.
  • 3On the next screen, tap Connect Stripe Account.
  • 4You will be redirected to Stripe's website. Enter the email address associated with your organization's Stripe account and continue.
  • 5Follow Stripe's prompts to authorize the connection.
  • 6Once connected, return to your RAYZIT back office. The Stripe status indicator will change from red to confirmed green.
TipIf your Stripe account was recently created, it may still be in verification. The connection will work but donations will be held until Stripe completes your identity verification — usually 1 to 2 business days.

4
Set Your Donation Limits
Customize
What This Step Is For

RAYZIT supports three types of giving: Round-Up donations, Regular recurring donations, and One-Off single donations. This step lets you choose which types to offer and set the suggested amounts your donors will see when they give.

How to Set Your Donation Options
  • 1In your back office menu, tap Donation Settings.
  • 2For One-Off donations: Toggle the switch to on. Set four suggested amounts that make sense for your organization — for example $10, $15, $20, and $50.
  • 3For Regular (recurring) donations: Toggle the switch to on. Set your suggested monthly amounts. These are gifts donors commit to on a monthly basis.
  • 4For Round-Up donations: Toggle the switch to on. Set a minimum round-up amount. We recommend $2 as a starting minimum. Round-Up donations happen automatically every time a donor makes a purchase with their connected card.
  • 5Tap Save when you are done. Your donation options are now live on your giving page.
TipRound-Up is the most powerful giving type on RAYZIT. Donors connect once and give passively every time they spend. Prioritize making sure this is turned on and set up correctly.

5
Add a Personalized Video to Your Homepage
Optional
What This Step Is For

Your RAYZIT homepage can display a YouTube video that plays automatically for anyone who visits your giving page. A short welcome message from your organization's leader creates personal connection with donors and significantly increases conversion.

How to Add Your Video
  • 1Upload your welcome video to YouTube. It does not need to be public — unlisted is fine.
  • 2Open the YouTube video and look at the URL in your browser. It will look something like this: youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ. The characters after v= are your Video ID.
  • 3Copy just those characters — nothing before or after them.
  • 4In your RAYZIT back office, tap Brand Settings.
  • 5Find the field labeled YouTube Video ID and paste your copied characters into it.
  • 6Tap Save. Your video will now appear on your giving page.
TipKeep the video under 90 seconds. A leader saying who you are, what you do, and why it matters is all you need. You can always come back and update it later.

6
Create Your First Event
Activate
What This Step Is For

Events are targeted fundraising campaigns on RAYZIT. Creating an event gives donors a specific reason to give right now, tied to a name, image, goal, and date. Named campaigns convert significantly better than general giving pages because they create urgency and context.

How to Create an Event
  • 1In your back office menu, tap Events.
  • 2Tap Create New Event.
  • 3Enter your Event Name. Keep it clear and specific — for example "Spring Fundraiser 2026" or "Back to School Drive."
  • 4Add an Event Description that tells donors what their money supports.
  • 5Upload an Event Image that represents the campaign. This appears at the top of your event giving page.
  • 6Set your Donation Amounts for this specific event.
  • 7Set your Start Date.
  • 8Tap Create. Your event page is now live and ready to share.
TipYou do not need a physical event to create an Event in RAYZIT. A "Spring Campaign" or "Monthly Challenge" works just as well. The name gives donors a rallying point.

7
Download Your QR Code
Activate
What This Step Is For

Your RAYZIT QR code is a direct link to your donation page. Anyone who scans it with their phone is taken instantly to your giving options — no app download required, no login needed. This is one of your most powerful tools for in-person giving at events, meetings, and gatherings.

How to Download Your QR Code
  • 1In your back office menu, tap Download QR Code.
  • 2Your QR code will download as a PNG image file to your device.
  • 3Print it and display it anywhere donors can see it — meeting rooms, event tables, presentation slides, bulletins, newsletters, and social media posts.
  • 4When a donor scans it, they are taken directly to your RAYZIT giving page with all your donation options ready.
TipYour general QR code links to your main giving page. Your Event pages each have their own separate QR code. Use the general one for ongoing promotion and the event-specific one during active campaigns.

You're Now Live — Begin Your 7-Day Launch
Final Step
What This Step Is For

Your RAYZIT account is fully set up and live. This final step transitions you from setup into activation. The 7-Day Launch is a structured outreach plan that gets your first donors connected in your first week. Organizations that follow the launch plan consistently outperform those that go live without one.

What Happens in Your 7-Day Launch
  • 1Day 0 — Inner Circle: Reach out to your most passionate supporters first. These are the people most likely to give immediately and help build early momentum before your public launch.
  • 2Day 1 — Public Launch: Send your first public announcement with a clear call to action. Share your RAYZIT link and QR code broadly across email, text, and social media.
  • 3Day 2 — SMS Follow-Up: Send a short direct text message to anyone who did not respond to Day 1. Keep it brief and action-focused.
  • 4Days 3 through 7: Continue with daily touchpoints using the assets and templates provided in your 7-Day Launch guide. Each day has a specific action designed to build on the last.
TipThe first 7 days set the tone for your entire RAYZIT experience. Organizations that launch with a plan generate donors in week one. Those that skip it often stay inactive. Do not skip the launch.
