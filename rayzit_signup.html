To receive donations through your RAYZIT account, your organization must also have a Stripe account. Stripe is how donations are securely transferred to your bank. If your organization does not currently have one, follow the steps below to create it.
Steps to Create Your Stripe Account
  • 1Visit www.stripe.com and click Get Started
  • 2Create your account using your organization's email address
  • 3Follow Stripe's onboarding steps to verify your organization
  • 4If your organization qualifies as a nonprofit, identify it as such during setup
  • 5Once your Stripe account is active, you are ready for Step 2
TipVerification for a Stripe account can sometimes take 1-2 business days. Complete this step as early as possible so it does not delay your account creation.
