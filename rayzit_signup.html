To receive donations through your RAYZIT account, your organization must also have a Stripe account. Stripe is how donations are securely transferred to your bank account. If your organization does not currently have one, follow the steps below to create it.

Important note
If you already have a Stripe account, you'll still need to create a separate account for RAYZIT donations using a different email. Stripe's "Legal Entity Sharing" lets multiple Stripe accounts use the same legal entity and EIN. When you create the second account with the same credentials, Stripe will prompt you to link it to your existing legal entity instead of re-entering the information.
Steps to create your Stripe account
  1. 1 Visit www.stripe.com and click Get Started
  2. 2 Create your account using your organization's email address
  3. 3 Follow Stripe's onboarding steps to verify your organization
  4. 4 If your organization qualifies as a nonprofit, identify it as such during setup
  5. 5 Once your Stripe account is active, you are ready for Step 2
Tip
Stripe verification typically takes 1-2 business days. Plan ahead if you're launching soon.
